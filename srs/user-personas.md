# SmartStream User Personas

This document outlines the primary user personas for the SmartStream Integrated Business Management System. These personas represent the target users across all five identified system roles.

## 1. The Director (Executive Management)
* **Name:** Mr. K.T. Dias
* **Role:** Director / Business Owner
* **Demographics:** 52 years old, frequently travels for business, relies heavily on his iPad and laptop.
* **System Interactions:** Requires a high-level view of operations, easy-to-read dashboards, financial reports, and remote access. Not highly involved in technical daily data entry.
* **Goals:** Wants real-time visibility into overall company performance, total inventory value, and live distributor locations. Needs to make quick, data-driven financial decisions.
* **Pain Points & Frustrations:** Highly frustrated by the current manual system where billing delays occur because of lost paper invoices. Hates waiting for end-of-week manual reports to know how the business is performing.

## 2. The Distributor (Field Operations)
* **Name:** Kamal Fernando
* **Role:** Distributor / Delivery Driver
* **Demographics:** 29 years old, works out in the field 90% of the day. Uses a mid-range Android smartphone.
* **System Interactions:** Requires a simplified mobile UI, GPS tracking capabilities, and easy photo upload features for invoices.
* **Goals:** Wants to complete his delivery routes quickly without being slowed down by complicated paperwork. Needs a mobile app that is fast, easy to navigate with one hand, and doesn't drain his battery.
* **Pain Points & Frustrations:** Currently frustrated by the responsibility of keeping track of physical paper invoices, which frequently get lost or damaged in transit. Dislikes typing long customer or product details on a small mobile screen.

## 3. The Stock Manager (Warehouse Operations)
* **Name:** Nuwan Perera
* **Role:** Stock Manager / Inventory Staff
* **Demographics:** 35 years old, stationed at the main KTD Lanka warehouse. Uses a desktop computer and a tablet.
* **System Interactions:** Responsible for maintaining stock levels. Needs rapid data entry, barcode/OCR support, and low-stock alerts.
* **Goals:** Needs to ensure the warehouse never runs out of popular items. Wants the system to automatically notify him via SMS/Email when supplies drop below a certain threshold so he doesn't have to check manually.
* **Pain Points & Frustrations:** Currently struggles with the static, manual Excel-based tracking system. Extremely frustrated by the constant discrepancies between what the Excel sheet says and the actual physical stock on the shelves.

## 4. The Accountant (Finance Department)
* **Name:** Sanduni Silva
* **Role:** Accountant
* **Demographics:** 31 years old, works in the main office, highly proficient in Excel and accounting software. 
* **System Interactions:** Needs accurate financial data, automated invoice processing, and seamless integration with payment tracking. 
* **Goals:** Wants to eliminate manual data entry. Hopes the new AI OCR system will automatically extract product names and totals from distributor uploads so she can approve them with one click.
* **Pain Points & Frustrations:** Wastes hours every week manually typing data from crumpled, hard-to-read paper invoices into the system. Frustrated by billing delays caused when distributors forget to hand in their delivery slips on time.

## 5. The System Admin (IT Support)
* **Name:** Shanuka Wijesinghe
* **Role:** System Administrator
* **Demographics:** 27 years old, tech-savvy, works from a dual-monitor desktop setup.
* **System Interactions:** Responsible for managing roles, access control (RBAC), and monitoring cloud backups.
* **Goals:** Wants a reliable, automated system. Needs to easily assign different permission levels (e.g., ensuring a Distributor cannot access the Director's financial dashboard). Wants peace of mind that the cloud disaster recovery backups are running successfully every 12 hours.
* **Pain Points & Frustrations:** Dislikes systems that are hard to maintain or require system downtime for minor updates. Frustrated when users share passwords, making strict role-based access control (RBAC) difficult to enforce.
